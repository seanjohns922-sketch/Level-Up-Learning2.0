# Chance Hollow Level 7 assessment review

Five parallel forms of 30 questions (150 items) at `/demo-review/chance-level7`. The demo review selector opens Pre-Test, Post-Test, Start, Mid and End. These are review banks; this change does not replace student diagnostic banks.

Source: the user's `mathematics-curriculum-content-7-10-v9.docx`, Year 7 Probability. AC9M7P01 covers single-stage sample spaces, assigning probabilities and predicting relative frequencies. AC9M7P02 covers large repeated experiments and digital simulations, comparing predictions with observations and explaining differences.

| Questions | Code | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–5 | AC9M7P01 | Complete sample spaces, distinct recorded outcomes, events and favourable outcomes |
| 6–12 | AC9M7P01 | Equally likely individual outcomes, unequal categories, complementary probabilities, exhaustive probabilities summing to one |
| 13–16 | AC9M7P01 | Predicted relative frequency, expected counts and probability inferred from a prediction |
| 17–19 | AC9M7P02 | Pool results using total trials; calculate and compare observed proportions |
| 20–23 | AC9M7P02 | Die simulation to 6000 trials, long-run behaviour, expected/observed difference, fixed trial-count design |
| 24–27 | AC9M7P02 | Digital model, spinner simulation to 8000 trials, variation and evidence of bias |
| 28–30 | AC9M7P02 | Equal theoretical models with differing observations, complete recording, evaluating a prediction |

The six coverage blocks are assessment planning groups, not a claim that Year 7 weekly lessons are built. Existing Chance Hollow activities cover Levels 3–6; Year 7 weekly lessons remain future work. This map should guide that lesson work. No Year 8 compound-event sample spaces or conditional-probability calculations are introduced.

There are 28 four-option questions and two genuine cumulative simulations per form. Die snapshots use 60, 600 and 6000 rolls; spinner snapshots use 80, 800 and 8000 spins. The final counts are scored against actual generated results, not expected counts. Increasing trials usually stabilises proportions; no claim says every extra trial improves accuracy or removes variation.

Reuse shared lesson apparatus, Australian coins, labelled outcome tiles, data tables and the random-number illustration. Every item has a visual or data display. All prose options begin with capitals, fractions use the shared formatter and read-aloud narrates visible evidence. Spinner sections and ticket counts match the data. Navigation and completion totals derive from form length, preserving 20-question Levels 3–6.

Validation: independent arithmetic and numerical-equivalence checks; correct-score and incorrect-score paths; complete visual/read-aloud evidence; cumulative simulation boundaries and actual-result scoring; both codes and all 150 identities. Browser QA covers all five forms, 30-question navigation, response retention, simulations and phone/tablet layouts.
