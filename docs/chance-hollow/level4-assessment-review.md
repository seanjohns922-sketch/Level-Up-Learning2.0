Five parallel forms with 20 questions each are available at `/demo-review/chance-level4`. This is the content-review bank; live student banks are unchanged pending review.

The programme's Week 3 and Week 6 curriculum tags were corrected: dependence is P01 and repeated experiments are P02. Lesson content is unchanged.

Level 4 adds three-way likelihood comparisons, replacement reasoning, two-coin outcomes and 16–24 trial results. It does not require fraction notation or numerical probability. Multiple-choice questions have four distinct options based on common misconceptions.

The approved Chance Hollow lesson apparatus renderer is reused for bags, spinners, dice and coins. Side-by-side diagrams show before/after draws and bag comparisons. Read-aloud narrates every visible caption, result and table. Two-coin trials sample HH, mixed and TT with weights 1:2:1; mixed includes HT and TH. Spinner trials use the displayed section proportions. Students record their actual outcomes, so no random split is required to earn the mark.

Checks cover all 100 items, answer options, row totals, weighted sampling boundaries, structured recording, both curriculum codes, all six lesson weeks and Level 3 regression. Browser checks cover all forms, interactions, navigation and phone/tablet widths.
