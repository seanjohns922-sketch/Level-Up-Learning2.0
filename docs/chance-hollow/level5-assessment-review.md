# Chance Hollow Level 5 assessment review

Five parallel forms of 20 questions are available at `/demo-review/chance-level5`. These are for content review; current student banks are unchanged pending approval.

Checked against the supplied Australian Curriculum v9 F–6 document (Year 5 Probability) and existing Chance Hollow Level 5 lessons and apparatus.

| Questions | Code | Evidence | Lesson week |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | AC9M5P01 | Complete outcome sets; individual versus grouped outcomes | 1 |
| 4–6 | AC9M5P01 | Equal sections versus unequal colour groups; repeated labels on a die | 2 |
| 7–9 | AC9M5P01 | All 36 dice pairs; unequal differences; repairing a race | 3 |
| 10–13 | AC9M5P02 | Record frequencies; fractions of trials; equal-chance simulation; relative frequency | 4 |
| 14–17 | AC9M5P02 | Estimate likelihood; evidence versus proof; unequal-chance simulation | 5 |
| 18–20 | AC9M5P02 | Plan repeated comparisons and explain observed variation | 6 |

The bank reuses lesson spinner, bag and die artwork. The lessons' two-dice grid highlights a target result, which would cue assessment answers. The review instead renders all 36 differences in a neutral table with row/column dice labels and read-aloud, without highlighting a result.

Multiple-choice items offer four distinct choices based on misconceptions. Fractions use the shared stacked fraction formatter. The interactive experiments contain 24 equal-chance spins and 30 unequal-chance spins; the table is scored against the generated results. Recorded datasets range up to 100 trials, and comparisons include different totals to assess relative frequency rather than raw counts alone. Students are not marked against a required random split.

Checks: all 100 items, both codes and six lesson weeks; independent difference counts (6, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2); row totals; fractional answers; equal/unequal sampling boundaries; correct and incorrect responses; Level 3–4 regression; typecheck/lint; browser review across every form and desktop/tablet/phone layouts.
