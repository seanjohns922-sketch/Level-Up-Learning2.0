# Chance Hollow Level 8 assessment review

Five forms of 30 questions (150 items) at `/demo-review/chance-level8`. Pre-Test, Post-Test, Start, Mid and End are linked from Demo Review. These are content-review forms; student banks are unchanged.

Checked against the user's `mathematics-curriculum-content-7-10-v9.docx`, Year 8 Probability.

| Questions | Code | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–6 | AC9M8P01 | Complements as percentages, fractions and decimals; unequal outcomes; exhaustive complementary events |
| 7–12 | AC9M8P02 | Tree diagrams, ordered pairs, all combinations, exactly one, at least one and at most one |
| 13–16 | AC9M8P02 | Venn diagrams: intersection, inclusive or, exclusive or, neither |

Shared lesson apparatus, Australian coin conventions, forecast cards and dice grids are reused. New tree and Venn renderers show explicit labels and region counts without highlighting an answer. All visible evidence is covered by the adjacent read-aloud control. Prose starts with capitals and fractions use the shared stacked formatter. The six coverage blocks are planning groups, not completed Year 8 weekly lessons; those remain future work.

Each form has 25 four-option questions, three diagram-completion tasks and two interactive simulations. Question 7 completes missing tree branches and an ordered pair; Question 8 completes a two-way sample-space table; Question 13 assigns all four coin pairs to labelled Venn regions. Completion choices are marked only when every field is answered, and each field must be correct for the question to score. Read-aloud describes visible blanks without exposing their answers. The coin simulation generates two independent fair tosses per trial, preserving HH, HT, TH and TT separately, with 40/400/4000 cumulative snapshots. The dice simulation generates two independent fair rolls and records their absolute difference, with 60/600/6000 snapshots. Both score students against actual generated results, never against an exact theoretical split.

Validation includes all 150 items and three curriculum codes, equivalent-choice checks, independent calculations from diagrams and tables, exhaustive enumeration of all 4 coin and 36 dice pairs, actual-result scoring and invalid/incomplete-response rejection. Browser checks cover all five forms, navigation, input retention, simulations, phone/tablet layouts and Demo Review menu access. Shared code retains Levels 3–7 behaviour.
