# Chance Hollow Level 3 assessment review

Chance starts at Level 3. Five parallel forms (Pre-Test, Post-Test, Start, Mid, End) each contain 20 questions. These are available at `/demo-review/chance-level3`; current student banks are unchanged pending content approval.

Checked against the supplied Australian Curriculum v9 F–6 document, Year 3 Probability, and the six-week Chance Hollow programme:

| Questions | Curriculum | Evidence | Lesson weeks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–4 | AC9M3P01 | Certain, impossible, likely and unlikely with bags and spinners | 1 |
| 5, 8 | AC9M3P01 | Everyday chance and reasons for predictions | 2 |
| 6–7 | AC9M3P01 | Possible outcomes and why an outcome is impossible | 3 |
| 9–10 | AC9M3P01 | Predictions and repeated draws with replacement | 4 |
| 11–16 | AC9M3P02 | Record and conduct trials; compare results | 5 |
| 17–20 | AC9M3P02 | Conduct trials and recognise/explain variation | 6 |

The existing lesson `ChanceVisual` renderer supplies controlled spinner sections, counters, coins and dice. Visible captions identify the evidence and nearby read-aloud includes trial results and table values. No apparatus highlights the correct response.

Each form has four distinct options on multiple-choice items, two results-recording tasks, and two interactive experiments. Experiments use genuine random trials, preserve outcomes when navigating between questions, and grade the student's record of those outcomes. An unusual random result is never marked wrong. Expected answers remain hidden behind review details.

Year 3 focuses on qualitative reasoning and observed results, without importing fraction probability or formal numerical probability from later levels. Question numbers, Back/Next, form switching and skip controls follow the existing review pattern. Theme tokens come from Chance Hollow.
