# Realmies Phase R3A Manufacturing Artwork Brief

All entries are concepts with `approval_status=pending`. The first four Number
Nexus entries are the review batch. The remaining eleven define manufacturing
constraints but their visual concepts must not be finalised before owner review.

Shared dimensions, face rules, material direction and approval gates are defined
in `docs/REALMIES_PHYSICAL_AND_DIGITAL_STYLE_GUIDE.md`.

The first family exploration is recorded in
`docs/REALMIES_NUMBER_NEXUS_CONCEPT_REVIEW.md`. The simple smiling mouth, inset
oval eyes with one upper-left highlight, broader feet and low-profile Number Nexus
base are now the approved family style. The generated sheets do not replace the
required individual front, three-quarter, side and back turnarounds.

## Number Nexus: first concept batch

### 1. Bitling

- **Stable key:** `number-nexus-bitling-standard`
- **Realm / type / rarity:** Number Nexus / realm citizen / common
- **Personality / job:** playful and curious; repairs broken numbers travelling
  through Number Nexus
- **Simplified body:** rounded-square head fused to a small bean body; short arms;
  two broad wedge feet
- **Proportions:** 47% head, 33% torso, 20% feet; target 44 mm high
- **Silhouette:** compact spark with one stepped corner on the head
- **Stance / contacts:** symmetrical stance; both full-width feet contact the ground
- **Defining feature:** one large rounded number cube integrated into the centre of
  the torso; no separate hip tile
- **Colour zones:** 4: navy body, cyan face panel, mint core, amber tile accent
- **Paint notes:** eyes and mouth share one mask; cube digit is shallow relief, not
  required to understand the figure
- **Hidden discovery:** `1010` as optional shallow relief on the side or back of
  the head
- **Split direction:** front/back; keep arms against torso
- **Undercuts:** shallow arm-to-body channels only
- **Breakage risk:** stepped corner and tile edge require 1.5 mm fillets
- **Manufacturing risk:** Low
- **Required simplification:** no pixels, floating tile or light effects
- **Hero pose:** feet planted, body shortened and head tilted slightly with playful
  energy
- **Front / side / back:** clean face panel front; torso cube reads in side profile;
  back uses one broad recessed circuit path
- **Grey prototype:** verify stepped head still reads without colour

### Number Nexus family review

- **Family resemblance:** rounded digital geometry, inset expressive eyes, compact
  head-led proportions, broad feet increased by approximately 5-10%, simple smiling
  mouths and simple colour blocking.
- **Unique silhouettes:** stepped spark, stacked carrier, archive capsule, shield guardian.
- **Likely sculpt conversion:** Bitling, CarryBot and Sentinel are direct block-outs;
  Codekeeper requires one archive-tab release study.
- **Expected digital appearance:** matte toy-like renders with soft cyan edge light;
  rarity is communicated by restrained finish and colour, not larger scale.
- **Shared base:** place all four on the same rounded Number Nexus hexagonal digital
  platform with the approved footprint and approximately 10% thinner visual profile.
  Keep connector design out of concept art until engineering review.
