# Realmies Phase R3 Production Artwork Brief

## Shared art direction

Create 15 transparent PNG collectible figures with:

- one consistent premium 3D-toy illustration style
- matching three-quarter camera angle and eye line
- matching soft studio key light and subtle rim light
- full-body framing and consistent apparent scale
- clean, readable silhouettes at 96 px
- transparent background
- no text, frame, card, badge or environment
- no pedestal unless all 15 use the same pedestal
- no resemblance that could confuse them with Numbot, Meazurex, Geospin or Datara

Recommended master canvas: 2048 x 2048 RGBA, with the figure contained inside a
1600 x 1800 central safe area.

## Per-asset production requirements

The following fields apply to every entry above:

- **Transparency:** true alpha transparency; no coloured matte, shadow rectangle
  or environment.
- **Camera:** matching eye-level three-quarter front view, facing slightly toward
  the centre of the canvas.
- **Scale:** full body occupies approximately 78% of canvas height; feet or lowest
  body point share a common baseline; no character may visually read at Legend scale.
- **Lighting:** matching soft studio key from upper left, subtle cool rim light
  and a small contact shadow contained within the alpha asset.
- **Crop safety:** all anatomy, props, glow and ribbons remain inside the central
  1600 x 1800 safe area.
