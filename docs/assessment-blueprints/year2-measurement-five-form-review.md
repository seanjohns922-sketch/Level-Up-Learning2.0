# Measurelands Level 2: five matched review forms

Source: supplied Australian Curriculum v9 F–6 PDF, pages 23–24.
Existing pre/post skills audited and retained where aligned. Twenty items per form;
same code, skill, response mode and intended difficulty across all five forms,
with changed examples, positions and choices. Fixed facts necessarily recur.

- AC9M2M01: uniform informal units; length, mass and capacity differences; smaller units for accuracy.
- AC9M2M02: equal halves, quarters and eighths in objects.
- AC9M2M03: dates, weekday lookup, elapsed days and two-week calendar jumps.
- AC9M2M04: analogue hour, half past, quarter past and quarter to.
- AC9M2M05: quarter, half, three-quarter and full turns.

Five easy, twelve moderate, three challenging. Challenge stays within Year 2.
Difficulty is an author judgement, not statistically calibrated. Practical and
oral assessment complements these selected/numeric responses.

All items have visuals: lesson clocks and object artwork, shared ribbons, dated
calendars, accurate fraction partitions and turn paths. Read-aloud covers prompts,
choices and diagram wording without announcing clock answers. IDK and previous
question navigation use the shared assessment controls.

Readable time choices replace four-digit time entry. Long misconception stories
become concise illustrated tasks. Calendar questions now show actual dates.
