## Coverage and difficulty

All nine assigned codes appear (N01–N06, A01–A03). A code on an item does not establish coverage of every verb in the descriptor. Not assessed by these forms, and to be reported as not assessed rather than zero:

- N01: number lines and hundreds-chart positions.
- N02: renaming (for example 245 as 24 tens and 5 ones) beyond slot 4's non-standard partition.
- N03: halves of collections.
- N04: bar models and writing a number sentence for a story.
- N05: naming arrays both ways (3 fours = 4 threes) and writing repeated-addition sentences.
- N06: multiplicative money, remainders, calendar problems; choosing and justifying an operation is sampled only in slot 15.
- A01: creating and describing a pattern.
- A02: fluent recall (timed) of addition facts to 20.
- A03: recall of twos multiplication facts.

The blueprint mix is retained in every form: difficulty 8 easy / 9 moderate / 3 challenging; cognitive demand 3 recall / 6 understanding / 7 application / 4 reasoning / 0 transfer; at most 6 selected responses and at least 14 constructed or manipulated. Difficulty labels are authoring judgements, not empirical estimates.

## Five-form plan

Build Pre-Test, Post-Test, Start, Mid and End as v3 forms from this corrected pair, one blueprint per slot:

- Same descriptor, subskill, response format, visual type, option structure, number range, regrouping pattern and step count in every form; different, independently solved examples.
- No form reuses a weekly-quiz example or another form's example for the same slot.
- Pre and Start are not easier than Post and End.
- New visuals never show the answer, the finished parts, or pre-drawn groups when grouping is the task.

## Preservation and release boundary

- The v2 banks and their IDs, prompts, keys and visuals stay byte-for-byte unchanged, so existing attempts, drafts and diagnostic sittings keep resolving v2.
- New pre-tests use v3; a post-test uses the version of the student's recorded baseline; diagnostic cycles are pinned and later checkpoints inherit the cycle's version.
- v2 and v3 are separate comparison groups; results are never compared across them as growth.
- Release needs version-safe routing. Level 1 and Prep each carry bespoke version code (`lib/year1-number-assessment-version.ts`, `lib/ground-number-assessment-version.ts`, a `number_level1_bank_version` column); Level 2 should instead use a shared realm-and-level version registry, with Level 1 and Prep routing proven unchanged.
- Renderer fixes that change what a question demands (removing a giveaway) apply only to v3 visuals. Presentation-only fixes (unit sizes, the $20 note) may apply to both.
