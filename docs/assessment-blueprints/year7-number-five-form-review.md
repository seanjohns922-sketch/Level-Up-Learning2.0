# Number Nexus Level 7 assessment blueprint

Thirty questions per form: Pre-Test, Post-Test, Start, Mid and End. All five are review-only until owner approval and a separately verified student release. Corresponding slots use the same curriculum code, response format and intended mathematical demand, with different examples.

## Sources

The supplied Australian Curriculum v9 documents are the source: `mathematics-curriculum-content-7-10-v9.docx` (Year 7 Number content descriptions and elaborations) and `mathematics-scope-and-sequence-f-10-v9.docx` (progression context). This is Year 7 Number, not a whole Year 7 Mathematics assessment. Algebra, Measurement, Space, Statistics and Probability belong to their respective realm blueprints.

## Difficulty controls

Each form has the same slot structure and intended difficulty profile. Accessible items check essential fluency; later multi-step tasks distinguish stronger understanding. Fraction arithmetic uses identical denominator relationships across forms: addition/subtraction in sevenths and quarters, multiplication in quarters and sevenths, and division by three-quarters. All inputs are positive for these fraction-operation items. Integer multiplication and division, formal exponent laws and irrational-number classification are reserved for the Year 8 blueprint.

Thirty items provide additional samples, not automatic measurement precision. Difficulty labels are author judgements, not calibrated item statistics. Familiarity with particular squares and factors, cancellation opportunities and number-line positions can still affect performance. Pilot completion time, omission rates and item performance before claiming equivalent psychometric difficulty. Manual review should also check fatigue at 30 questions.

## Wording and presentation

Prompts are at most 16 words. Mathematical notation is large, including stacked fractions and superscripts. Square-area and signed-number-line diagrams show the required information without worked answers. Counter colours have written labels and totals. Paint tins, packs, mixing ratios, backpack delivery and fundraiser costs use labelled quantities and units. No shaded fraction models or worked unit costs reveal the requested calculation. Read-aloud includes contextual quantities but does not name the marked point on the number line. Profit questions use different figures so one does not provide the other's answer.

Numeric responses clearly request their units; fractional calculations use four answer choices to avoid requiring slash entry on tablet number keyboards. The owner can switch forms at the same slot, navigate all 30 questions, reveal answers, and return to Demo Review. Review responses remain in component state and do not write student results.

## Evidence limits and release boundary

These items sample the descriptors. Selecting a model does not independently demonstrate a student's full explanation, strategy selection, digital-tool use or justification. Classroom and lesson evidence remains necessary.

Before student release, extend the supported diagnostic range, version pinning, save/resume, expected question counts, score denominators and reporting to Level 7 and 30 questions. Do not route these banks into the existing 20-question student workflow or extrapolate a Level 7 score from an F–6 result. The existing tracker already displays Prep–8; its scale alone is not evidence that Level 7 assessments are integrated.
