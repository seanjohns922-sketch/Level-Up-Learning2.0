# Level 7 Measurelands: five matched forms

Status: manual review; not released into student assessment cycles.

## Source

The user's supplied `/Users/seanjohns/Downloads/mathematics-curriculum-content-7-10-v9.docx`, Year 7 Measurement descriptions and elaborations, AC9M7M01–06. This is the same source previously used for Level 7 Number Nexus. The scope document supplied alongside it is available for progression context. The current repository's Measurelands weekly lessons end at Year 6; no Level 7 lesson sequence was assumed to exist.

Each form has 20 questions (100 total), with five accessible, ten moderate and five challenging items. These are intended difficulty judgements, not calibrated results. Sixteen responses are typed numbers and four require choosing a relationship, explanation or justified plan. Pre-Test, Post-Test, Start, Mid and End assess matched skills with varied quantities and rotated choices.

The short assessments sample these descriptors. They do not replace the practical investigations, open-ended modelling or teacher-observed explanations in the full curriculum. No money questions are included.

## Presentation and reuse

Inspected the existing Measurelands area, volume, volume-builder and angle lesson components before authoring. Rectangular prisms reuse the lesson volume engine's projection bounds with solid faces rather than countable unit cubes. Existing Year 7 drink and paint artwork supports ratio contexts. Other diagrams use controlled SVG geometry for triangles, parallelograms, triangular prisms, circles and transversals; angle geometry is calculated from the labelled values.

Dimensions sit outside shapes. Height lines have right-angle markers. Parallel lines have matching arrow marks and the exact angle sectors are highlighted. Isosceles sides have matching ticks. Unknown dimensions and angles are excluded from narration. All written instructions, labels and given quantities are covered by Read diagram, and the shared prompt/option narration and I don't know controls remain available. Measurelands theme and standard level dropdown are used.
