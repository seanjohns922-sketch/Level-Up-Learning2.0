# Number assessments: Ground through Level 7

16 September 2026. Approved Ground–6 forms have 20 items; Level 7 has 30. This release connects the approved Level 3 review bank and Level 7 forms. Existing Level 3 drafts/baselines retain v2; new pre-tests use v3. Post-tests match the baseline bank. Old diagnostic cycles retain their bank versions and maximum Level 6; new cycles use Level 3 v3 and may probe Number Level 7. Other strands remain at their published ceilings.

Level 7 pre/post tests are assessment extensions. Students with Level 6 Number placement can open **Level 7 Number tests** in the Number top bar or the 3D Realms travel panel. `/number-extension` links to the normal pre/post URLs. These record 30 answer snapshots in canonical assessment history, preserve learning-cycle pairing, deduplicate submission retries and leave weekly lesson placement untouched. The post-test requires a prior Level 7 baseline. A separate report in the student's Number detail displays extension growth and all saved answer replays.

Supervised diagnostics use 30 questions at Number Level 7, save indices 0–29 and validate distinct complete question sets. New-cycle ceiling and old-cycle boundaries are enforced in SQL and the client. Level 7 Number evidence appears in the per-strand tracker. The whole-maths primary aggregate remains on its existing F–6 scale; Number extension evidence does not redefine the other five strands. Lesson placement remains capped at released Year 6 lessons, with the extension flag after mastery.

Migration: `20260916090000_number_level3_and_level7_release.sql`. Level 8 is a protected five-form review only.
