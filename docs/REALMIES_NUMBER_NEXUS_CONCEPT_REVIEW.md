# Number Nexus Realmies Concept Review

## Scope

This review covers only:

1. Bitling
2. CarryBot
3. Codekeeper
4. Neon Sentinel

Measurelands, Starpath and Beyond the Fog remain paused.

This candidate keeps the simple smile direction while reducing surface detail and

- CarryBot: square worker body with an integrated place-value backpack
- Codekeeper: calm capsule body with an archive-key chest plate
- Neon Sentinel: broad protector body with an integrated shield chest

### Official family style reference

`/realmies/concepts/number-nexus-family-style-lock.png`

The owner approved the visual DNA at 9.3/10. This sheet applies the final controlled
family adjustments:

- feet broadened for physical stability
- base width retained with a thinner visual profile
- one identical upper-left eye highlight construction
- Neon Sentinel given broader shoulders and a slightly smaller head
- optional binary and number discoveries added as shallow secondary relief

Both sheets were generated with the same manufacturing-first brief:

- compact rounded proportions
- slightly oversized heads
- shared inset oval eye construction
- thick limbs and simple hands
- broad stable feet
- satin plastic finish
- 3-5 colour zones
- restrained poses
- low rounded-hexagonal Number Nexus bases
- no logo, universal mark, connector or fragile floating part

## Decision boundary

The images are family explorations, not production assets. The image generator
varied armour, proportions and small construction details between versions despite
the shared prompt. Those differences are not approved and must not influence the
mouth decision.

The shared family direction is now locked to simple smiling mouths. The eyes-only and
earlier mouth sheets remain design-history references. Individual turnarounds,
transparent production renders and physical engineering come later.

## Permanent identity rule

Realmies do not evolve. Bitling, CarryBot, Codekeeper and Neon Sentinel retain
their canonical names, jobs, defining features and core silhouettes. Finish or
colour variants may be explored later, but they are variants of the same timeless
Realmie rather than evolutionary forms.

The official style reference is the controlling visual source for all turnaround
work. Generated turnaround sheets remain concept handoff material until their
views are checked for cross-view consistency and approved for professional
sculpting.
