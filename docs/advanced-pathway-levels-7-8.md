# Advanced Pathway — Levels 7 and 8

Status: **deferred product direction**. Finish and validate the current Whole-Maths
Diagnostic before beginning this work.

## Purpose

Level Up Learning's existing Prep–6 journey remains complete and unchanged. Levels
7 and 8 will provide a genuine continuation for high-achieving students who master
Level 6, rather than leaving them with a diagnostic score but no suitable learning
content.

Levels 7 and 8 must be curriculum-aligned secondary extension pathways. They must
not be produced by merely increasing the numbers or difficulty in Level 6 questions.

## Agreed learner experience

Each realm ends Level 6 with a special **Summit Challenge** card presented as the
realm's final boss:

1. The student completes the Level 6 pathway.
2. The Summit Challenge assesses the essential Level 6 knowledge for that strand.
3. Mastery at the canonical assessment threshold makes the student eligible for the
   Advanced Pathway in that strand.
4. A teacher confirms a primary student's first move into secondary content.
5. Level 7 opens in a visually distinct extension area beyond the existing Prep–6
   journey.
6. Level 8 follows only after Level 7 is complete, audited and trialled.

Working name: **Advanced Pathway**. Possible learner-facing world language includes
“Beyond the Summit”, “The Higher Realm”, “Mastery Expedition” or “The Ascension
Path”. Naming is not yet final.

## Strand-specific progression

Advanced access is earned independently in each realm. A student may be working at
Level 7 in Number and Algebra while remaining at Level 6 in Measurement, Space,
Statistics or Probability.

The eventual Level 7 and Level 8 implementation must include the same complete
learning and reporting contract as the existing levels:

- curriculum-mapped weekly lessons
- weekly quizzes
- independent pre-tests and post-tests
- secure progression and persistence
- live-progression calculations
- Whole-Maths Diagnostic integration
- teacher and parent reporting

## Diagnostic relationship

The Whole-Maths Diagnostic must eventually be extended from a Prep–6 continuum to a
Prep–8 continuum so it can measure achievement above Level 6. A high result must not
produce an advanced label without a corresponding learning destination.

Diagnostic evidence may identify readiness and allow a teacher to assign the
Advanced Pathway without making a student repeat content they have already mastered.
One strong result alone should not silently move a primary student into secondary
content; the first transition requires teacher confirmation.

Until Levels 7 and 8, their assessments and the extended scoring model are built and
released, the official Whole-Maths Diagnostic remains capped at Level 6.

## Curriculum direction

The content must be mapped directly to Australian Curriculum Mathematics v9 for
Years 7 and 8 across all six existing strands: Number, Algebra, Measurement, Space,
Statistics and Probability. Expected areas include integers and rational numbers,
ratios and rates, percentages, exponent notation and index laws, algebraic
expressions and linear equations, Cartesian graphs, geometric reasoning, circles
and prisms, statistical sampling and multi-step probability experiments.

## Delivery order

1. Finish and validate the existing Whole-Maths Diagnostic.
2. Map Year 7 curriculum descriptors and audit the progression architecture for a
   Prep–8 ceiling.
3. Design the Summit Challenge contract and teacher-approval gate.
4. Build and audit Level 7 across all six realms.
5. Integrate Level 7 into diagnostics, live progression and reporting.
6. Pilot Level 7 with high-achieving students.
7. Begin Level 8 only after Level 7 has been validated.

This document records product direction only. It does not authorise enabling Level
7 or Level 8 routes, placements, scoring or UI before their full release contract is
implemented and audited.
