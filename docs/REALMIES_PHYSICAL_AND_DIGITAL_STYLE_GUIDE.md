# Realmies Physical and Digital Style Guide

## Product vision

Realmies are small inhabitants discovered through learning. Each design must work
as both:

- a polished transparent collectible in My Realmies; and
- a sturdy, affordable 40-55 mm plastic figure suitable for professional sculpting.

Realmies are not Legends, Pets, avatars or achievement objects. Numbot, Meazurex,
Geospin and Datara remain Legend characters and must not be used as Realmies.

This guide is a concept and 3D-handoff standard. It does not certify a design as
manufacturing-ready. Final production requires professional sculpting, engineering,
material and child-safety review, mould-flow analysis, sampling and compliance tests.

## Originality standard

The collection uses an original "small realm inhabitant" language:

- compact head-led proportions rather than oversized black-eyed vinyl heads;
- realm-shaped body cores rather than a common licensed-character body;
- expressive inset eyes with visible iris or light-point detail;
- integrated tools and symbols that arise from the character's job;
- soft geometric forms shared across the collection without copying another toy system.

Do not imitate the proportions, eyes, bases, joints, silhouettes, packaging or
surface treatment of Ooshies, Funko, LEGO, Pokemon or another identifiable range.
Reference products may inform manufacturing constraints only.

## Shared proportions

- Head or main face volume: 40-50% of total height.
- Body: compact, with no narrow waist below 30% of head width.
- Arms: minimum apparent thickness 3.5 mm at final scale.
- Ankles, stems, antennae and attached tools: minimum 3 mm, preferably 4 mm.
- Hands: simplified and readable. Feet: broad, stable and approximately 5-10%
  larger than the initial concept proportions.
- Eye detail: broad inset shapes; no tiny raised pupils or separate eye pieces.
- Surface detail: shallow relief, engraving or paint rather than attached micro-parts.

Target envelope:

| Dimension | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Height | 40-55 mm |
| Width | no more than 40-45 mm |
| Depth | no more than 35-40 mm |
| Ground contacts | two broad feet or one continuous integrated footprint |

## Approved family signature: the design language

Realmies have no universal visible logo, chest core, tattoo, stamp or compulsory
body symbol. A character should be recognisable as a Realmie from the collection's
shared sculpting language:

- chunky, rounded and friendly primary forms;
- head-led proportions with a short compact body;
- one recognisable family of inset oval eyes with an identical single upper-left
  white highlight;
- small readable hands and strong feet;
- smooth plastic surfaces and simple colour blocking;
- slightly active but stable poses;
- clear front, side and shelf silhouettes;
- no tiny detail required to understand the character.

Eye shape may adapt to a character's face and personality, but uses the same
construction principles: inset eye wells, visible light-point or iris detail,
soft corners and a single accessible face paint plane. The eyes must not become
oversized solid-black vinyl-toy eyes.

Realm identity comes from each inhabitant's silhouette, job, colours and integrated
realm objects. It is not imposed through a repeated badge.

### Frozen design DNA

The approved Number Nexus family reference freezes the following rules for every
future Realmie:

- head-led compact proportions;
- inset oval eyes with one upper-left white highlight;
- simple friendly smiling mouths;
- simplified hands and broad stable feet;
- opaque satin plastic finish;
- bold 3-5 zone colour blocking;
- low-profile realm base thickness and shared footprint envelope;
- stable, slightly active poses;
- one clear silhouette and one integrated defining feature per character.

These rules may not be reinterpreted by later artwork sessions without an explicit
owner-approved design-system revision. New realms must express their identity
through character silhouettes, jobs, colours, integrated objects and realm bases,
not by changing the family DNA.

### No evolution rule

Realmies do not evolve. Their identity, name and core sculpt remain stable over
time. Cards, Legends and students may evolve; Realmies do not.

Future collectible variants may change finish or approved colour treatment on the
same character identity, but must not imply a stronger evolutionary stage or
replace the canonical Realmie design.

## Realm-specific display bases

The approved collection concept is a family of realm-specific bases:

- **Number Nexus:** rounded hexagonal digital platform
- **Measurelands:** stone measuring tile with broad engraved calibration marks
- **Starpath:** low rounded asteroid platform
- **Beyond the Fog:** broad swirling mist platform

These bases may eventually connect to form shelf displays. The connection system
is not approved production geometry yet. A toy engineer must determine safe
dimensions, tolerances, connection force, mould splits and whether bases are
integrated or separate.

Base rules:

- one consistent outer envelope and character baseline across all realms;
- keep the approved footprint width while using a low-profile base approximately
  10% thinner than the initial Number Nexus concepts;
- realm identity comes from broad shape and shallow relief, not printed text;
- rounded edges, no sharp asteroid points or thin mist curls;
- a figure remains visually complete without relying on its base artwork;
- no connector small enough to become a loose small part;
- digital heroes may include the base when presenting the physical collection,
  while character-only transparent renders remain available for UI flexibility.

## Form and stance

- Use one-piece-first construction.
- Keep the centre of mass above the footprint.
- Use neutral, confident poses with no running, jumping, hovering or one-foot balance.
- Join hands, props, ribbons, rings and effects back into the main body.
- Use two broad feet where possible, increased by approximately 5-10% from the
  initial family concepts; a continuous mist or root footprint is acceptable.
- Keep elbows and tools close to the torso to reduce undercuts.
- Design every figure to stand securely on its intended realm base.
- Where practical, the character block-out should also remain stable before the
  base connector is finalised.

## Materials and finish

Concept target: soft-matte opaque plastic with restrained satin highlights.

- Default body: one opaque mould colour.
- Maximum paint zones: 3-5, plus one small accent.
- Eyes and the approved simple smiling mouth should fit within one face paint mask where
  practical.
- Metallic, pearlescent or translucent effects are finish variants on the same mould,
  not new character geometry.
- Avoid effects that depend on gradients, bloom or internal illumination.
- Do not use flocking, glitter shedding or brittle clear projections in the base range.

## Face language

- Friendly and emotionally readable at arm's length.
- Two inset oval eyes with one identical upper-left white highlight; small brows
  may be used where they remain paint-only and manufacturing-safe.
- Use a simple friendly smiling mouth throughout the core collection. Keep it
  shallow, readable and compatible with the shared face paint plane.
- Avoid oversized solid-black eyes and hyper-detailed anime features.
- Avoid separate teeth, eyelashes, glasses or tiny face accessories.
- Keep the face on a single accessible paint plane.

## Hidden discovery details

Realmies may include one optional hidden number, binary value or realm clue:

- place it on a side, back, backpack or integrated chest feature;
- use shallow relief or engraving only;
- do not add a loose part, extra paint zone or fragile projection;
- keep it secondary so the character remains readable without finding it;
- use it as a close-looking reward, not as required gameplay information.

In the digital collection, an undiscovered character may display `?????` until
discovery reveals its canonical name. This is a product direction for the future
collection experience, not approval to change the current My Realmies UI in this
artwork phase.

## Mould and undercut rules

- Default to a simple front/back split direction.
- Side cores or extra slides require explicit cost justification.
- No floating parts, trapped loops, deep cavities or crossed limbs.
- Orbit rings, map edges, leaves, cloaks and light effects must merge into the body.
- Convert tiny graphics to shallow relief or paint.
- Keep concave gaps broad enough for tool access and paint masks.
- Any `High` manufacturing-risk concept is rejected from the approved standard.
- `Moderate` concepts require a grey prototype review before colour artwork.

```text
public/realmies/{stable-key}/colour-zones.png
public/realmies/{stable-key}/specification.md
```

Hero images are 2048 x 2048 RGBA. The figure stays within a central
1600 x 1800 safe area. No frame, text, scenery or baked floor shadow is allowed.
A contact shadow may be delivered as a separate layer.

Required review views:

1. front orthographic-style concept
2. front three-quarter hero
3. left side
4. right side
5. back
6. solid silhouette
7. transparent digital hero
8. unpainted neutral-grey prototype
9. numbered colour-zone guide

## 3D handoff

An approved concept package must identify:

- target dimensions and centre of mass;
- primary volumes and minimum thicknesses;
- intended ground contacts;
- split direction and likely parting line;
- integrated versus separate elements;
- paint zones and mask boundaries;
- areas that may need draft, fillets or thickening;
- known undercuts and proposed resolution;
- reference turnarounds with consistent proportions.

Artwork approval means `3D-modelling-ready`, not `manufacturing-ready`.

## Child-safety boundary

Realmies are intended for children, so concepts must avoid sharp points, pinch
features, brittle protrusions and detachable small accessories. Age grading,
chemical compliance, mechanical testing, small-parts testing, labelling and local
regulatory approval remain mandatory before physical release. This document is
not a safety certification.

## Approval checklist

Before a concept leaves `pending`, confirm:

- [ ] not a Legend or Pet
- [ ] original design with no confusing third-party resemblance
- [ ] recognisable at 96 px and as a solid silhouette
- [ ] stable at 40-55 mm
- [ ] no floating, excessively thin, sharp or tiny detached parts
- [ ] no meaning dependent on text, gradients or glow
- [ ] no more than five primary paint zones plus one accent
- [ ] plausible one-piece-first split
- [ ] manufacturing risk is Low or resolved Moderate
- [ ] complete front, three-quarter, side, back and grey views
- [ ] professional sculptor has enough information to begin a block-out
- [ ] sculpting language matches the approved Realmies family
- [ ] no universal visible mark has been introduced
- [ ] realm base concept is present without unapproved connector geometry
- [ ] naming review has been recorded
