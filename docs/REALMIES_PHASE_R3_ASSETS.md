# Realmies Phase R3 Assets

The old 18 Legend renders are retired from the Realmies product. They remain
Card/Hall of Legends artwork and must not be reused as collection figures.

The complete long-term collection still requires 15 transparent
supporting-creature renders. The authoritative brief is:

`docs/REALMIES_PHASE_R3_ARTWORK_BRIEF.md`

The manufacturing-first family standard and canonical concept manifest are:

- `docs/REALMIES_PHYSICAL_AND_DIGITAL_STYLE_GUIDE.md`
- `docs/realmies-r3a-asset-manifest.json`

R3A does not create final manufacturing artwork. The first review batch was
Bitling, CarryBot, Codekeeper and Neon Sentinel. Number Nexus family exploration
sheets established the mouth decision:

- `/realmies/concepts/number-nexus-family-mouths.png`
- `/realmies/concepts/number-nexus-family-eyes-only.png`
- `/realmies/concepts/number-nexus-family-refined-mouths.png`
- `/realmies/concepts/number-nexus-family-style-lock.png`

These are review references, not production renders or approved turnarounds.
The style-lock sheet is the approved visual DNA reference. It locks the shared
proportions, eye construction, simple smiles, hands, broad feet, satin finish,
manufacturing simplicity, pose family and colour-blocking discipline. It does not
approve final individual turnarounds or production sculpts. Generative differences
beyond an explicitly locked direction must not be interpreted as product DNA. The
family signature is the shared sculpting language; no universal visible core, mark
or footprint is permitted. Realm-specific connecting bases are a collection concept
with production engineering still pending.

The four Number Nexus turnaround concepts are:

- `/realmies/turnarounds/number-nexus-bitling-turnaround-concept.png`
- `/realmies/turnarounds/number-nexus-carrybot-turnaround-concept.png`
- `/realmies/turnarounds/number-nexus-codekeeper-turnaround-concept.png`
- `/realmies/turnarounds/number-nexus-neon-sentinel-turnaround-concept.png`

Each source sheet includes front, left, right, back, three-quarter hero,
neutral-grey prototype and colour-zone guidance.

Each model also has an STL concept export, metadata, transparent hero, front,
three-quarter, left, right, back, silhouette, grey prototype, colour-zone render
and written specification. The combined visual review is:

- `/realmies/3d/number-nexus-3d-review.png`

These are digital concept packages for owner approval and professional sculptor
handoff. They are deliberately not described as manufacturing-ready. Physical
production still requires watertight sculpt refinement, wall-thickness and
tolerance engineering, balance and drop testing, mould planning, material choice,
age grading and product-safety certification.

not manufacturing-ready sculpts.

Phases 2 and 3 now account for 8 of the planned 15 Realmies. Starpath and Fog
remain pending. The digital collection is released with Number Nexus and
Measurelands only. Starpath appears as a generic, non-interactive Coming Soon

digital release and all physical-production statuses remain pending.
