# Tower Realm Chamber Art Bible

## Identity

The Tower is universal Level Up Learning architecture, not a curriculum realm. Its permanent visual language is warm stone, bronze and gold mechanisms, monumental arches, circular floor geometry and restrained magical light. Realm colour is confined to portal frames, labels, floor rings and portal media.

## Composition

- Circular chamber with a clear Central World exit at the south edge.
- Suspended knowledge core at the centre as the primary landmark.
- Ten portals distributed around the perimeter from configuration.
- The entrance view shows the central core and far-side portals; side portals are discovered by walking and turning.
- Portal frames share one architectural silhouette. Posters, accent colour, subject and state provide realm identity.

## Portal States

- **Dormant:** static compressed poster, restrained ring and label.
- **Inside ring:** brighter frame/ring, one muted portal video mounted, explicit Enter action shown.
- **Coming Soon:** darkened poster, physical seal and text status. No video or entry.
- **Low quality/reduced motion:** static poster only.

## Interaction Rules

The visible floor ring is the activation boundary. Crossing it may start one preview video, but realm entry always requires an explicit button or Enter key. Leaving the ring unmounts the video, pauses media, releases its source and disposes the video texture.

## Performance Rules

- Portal posters are dedicated 480 x 720 JPEG crops, not full dashboard backgrounds.
- No portal video is part of the initial chamber payload.
- At most one video may play.
- Geometry remains procedural and low-poly until production models are separately approved.
- Quality tiers control DPR, antialiasing, column count and video availability.

## Production Art Handoff

Future portal models should preserve the existing footprint, ring radius, interaction anchor, label anchor and opening aspect ratio. Artists may replace frame geometry and sealed emblems without changing realm metadata, routing or progression code.
