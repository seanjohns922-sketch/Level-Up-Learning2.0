# Level Up Learning Delivery Sequence

**Agreed:** 5 August 2026

## Current State

- Measurelands Assessments v1.0 is live and frozen.
- The shared Assessment Framework is frozen.
- Assessment engines, routing, persistence, progression, replay, reporting,
  rewards and database contracts are not part of upcoming content rebuilds.

## Agreed Sequence

### 1. Complete Starpath Curriculum

Finish Starpath across every intended level before rebuilding its assessments.

Completion includes:

- curriculum-aligned lessons
- all planned weeks and levels
- weekly quizzes with exactly 15 questions in a 5-5-5 lesson split
- progression and navigation wiring
- cumulative level content and graduation flows
- curriculum, generation and route audits

Starpath Pre-Tests and Post-Tests remain provisional until this curriculum is
complete, audited and frozen.

### 2. Complete Number Nexus Overhaul

After Starpath curriculum completion, overhaul Number Nexus end to end.

The overhaul includes:

- curriculum coverage audit by level
- lesson-content corrections
- independent Lesson, Weekly Quiz and Assessment banks
- weekly quiz contract validation
- independent Pre-Test and Post-Test rebuilds under the frozen Assessment Framework
- progression, replay, reporting and placement regression audits

### 3. Rebuild Starpath Assessments

Once Starpath curriculum is frozen, rebuild its Pre-Tests and Post-Tests against
the final taught curriculum using the same Assessment Framework as Measurelands.

Do not author final Starpath assessment banks before the curriculum freeze. This
prevents descriptor drift, lesson-derived item reuse and repeated rework.

### 4. Cross-Realm Release Audit

Run a final audit across Measurelands, Number Nexus and Starpath covering:

- curriculum ownership and descriptor coverage
- lesson, quiz and assessment bank independence
- assessment routing and launch behavior
- canonical saving and replay
- teacher reporting and student insights
- placement and targeted pathways
- weekly and final-week progression
- XP, rewards and reveal isolation

## Immediate Next Step

Continue Starpath curriculum implementation. Do not begin the Number Nexus
overhaul or Starpath assessment rebuild until Starpath curriculum is complete.
