# Number Nexus Ground Assessment v1.0

**Status:** Production  
**Live from:** 6 August 2026

The Ground Number Nexus Post-Test resolves to the approved independent,
assessment-authored bank. The former generated Prep form is retained as a
deprecated legacy archive and is not selectable through the production resolver
or the general post-test registry.

The 85% pass threshold and the existing assessment engine, persistence, replay,
reporting, placement, progression, rewards and database contracts are unchanged.

The 20 production items remain `uncalibrated` until representative classroom
response data is available. Their canonical metadata supports later item
difficulty, discrimination, distractor and misconception analysis.
