# Bitling Digital Prototype Specification

- Canonical key: `number-nexus-bitling-standard`
- World job: Repairs lost numbers travelling through Number Nexus.
- Defining feature: Integrated lime number panel with a raised `3`.
- Hidden detail: Shallow `1010` relief on the back.
- Target display height: approximately 44 mm before professional fabrication review.
- Colour zones: cyan body, navy face frame and torso, lime panel and eyes, yellow numeral, pale blue base.
- Shared DNA: rounded proportions, inset oval eyes with identical upper-left highlights, simple smile, thick limbs, broad feet, satin plastic finish and low rounded-hexagonal base.

The GLB and STL are digital concept geometry for visual approval and downstream
sculpting. They are not manufacturing-ready. A professional must resolve watertight
unions, wall thickness, tolerances, centre of gravity, mould parting, material,
drop testing, age grading and product-safety certification.
