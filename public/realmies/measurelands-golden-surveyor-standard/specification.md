# Golden Surveyor Digital Prototype

- Target display height: 54 mm
- Defining feature: integrated folded-map relief and broad surveyor hood
- Shared DNA: locked Realmies eyes, smile, hands, broad feet, satin finish and base footprint
- Measurelands base: low stone measuring tile with broad calibration marks
- Status: digital concept prototype only

This model is not manufacturing-ready. A professional sculptor and manufacturer must resolve watertight geometry, wall thickness, tolerances, mould release, balance, material choice, paint masks and applicable toy-safety requirements.
